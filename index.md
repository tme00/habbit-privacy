# habbit Privacy Policy

Effective as of October 23, 2026.

habbit keeps everything you make on your phone. We have no accounts, no servers that hold your data, no analytics and no ads, so we never see your habits, counters, events or contacts.

Talal Eshki ("habbit", "we", "us" or "our") operates habbit, an iOS app for tracking habits, counters, events and birthdays (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, where it is kept, and the few cases where it leaves your device.

NOTICE TO EUROPEAN USERS: Please see the Notice to European Users section below for additional information for individuals in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom ("Europe").

## 1. Information the App uses

The App only uses information you enter or choose to give it. None of it is sent to us.

- **Content you create.** Habits, check-ins, streaks, goals, counters, events, reminders, journal notes, icons, and items you archive or delete (kept in Recently Deleted until removed).
- **Contacts (optional).** If you choose to import birthdays, the App asks for access to your contacts. It reads the names and birthdays of the contacts you pick and saves them in the App. You can decline and still use everything else.
- **Location (optional).** If you add a place to an event, the App may ask for your location while you use it. This centers the map and puts nearby places first in search results. The App never tracks your location in the background.
- **Notifications (optional).** If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders on your phone. They are created on your device; we do not use a push notification server.
- **Settings.** Your theme, the order of your lists, and whether you have seen the welcome guide.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, payment details, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or IP address.

## 2. Tracking technologies

The App does not use cookies, web beacons, analytics tools, advertising SDKs or any other tracking technology. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 3. How we use your information

The App uses your information only to provide its features on your device:

- showing your habits, counters, events and birthdays, and working out streaks and progress;
- scheduling the reminders you set up;
- showing your items in Home Screen widgets;
- finding places for events and opening them in Apple Maps or Google Maps when you tap to do so;
- creating and restoring backup files when you ask for one.

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, profiling or automated decision-making.

## 4. Where your information is stored and retention

Everything is stored in the App's storage on your iPhone. The Home Screen widgets read a copy through a shared storage area on the same phone; nothing goes through our servers.

- **Device backups.** Your App data is included in your iCloud or computer backups, under your Apple account and Apple's privacy terms. This lets your data move to a new phone.
- **Backup files.** "Export Backup" creates a file and opens the share sheet. You decide where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, or a message). We never receive it.
- **Retention.** Your data stays until you delete it. Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then are removed. Deleting the App removes its data from your phone, but not from backups or exported files you made.

## 5. How your information is shared

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Some features ask Apple or another provider to do something for you, and they send that provider only what the request needs:

- **Place search (Apple).** When you search for a place, your search text and, if you allowed location, your approximate position are sent to Apple Maps to find results. Turning a map point into an address also uses Apple's location services. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Opening maps.** If you tap to open a place in Apple Maps or Google Maps, that app or website receives the place's location and name. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Authorities.** Because we hold none of your data, we have nothing to hand over in response to legal requests.

## 6. Your choices

- **Permissions.** You can allow or turn off Contacts, Location and Notifications at any time in iOS Settings > habbit. The App keeps working without them.
- **View, change and delete.** You can edit or delete any item in the App, empty Recently Deleted, or delete the App to remove all of its data.
- **Take your data with you.** "Export Backup" in Settings gives you a copy of everything you made, in a readable JSON file.

## 7. Other sites and services

The App can open Apple Maps, Google Maps or their websites. We do not control those services, and their own privacy policies apply.

## 8. Security

Your data is protected by your iPhone's security, including its passcode and Apple's device encryption. Exported backup files are not encrypted by the App, so store and share them with care. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee security.

## 9. International data transfers

We do not transfer your information anywhere, because we never receive it. When you use place search, your query goes to Apple, which may process it outside your country under its own privacy policy.

## 10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Since all data stays on the device, we hold no information about any child. If you have questions, contact us.

## 11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example if we add new features or in-app purchases. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the App or on the App Store page. Using the App after a change means the updated policy applies.

## 12. How to contact us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, open an issue at [github.com/tme00/habbit-privacy/issues](https://github.com/tme00/habbit-privacy/issues).

## 13. Notice to European users

**Controller.** Talal Eshki is the controller for any personal data processed by the App, for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR. Contact details are in How to contact us above.

**Legal bases.** The App processes your data on your device only. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

| Purpose | Data involved | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Providing the App's features | Content you create, settings | Contractual Necessity |
| Importing birthdays | Names and birthdays of contacts you pick | Consent (iOS permission) |
| Finding nearby places | Search text, approximate location | Consent (iOS permission) |
| Reminders | Reminder times and text | Consent (iOS permission) |

**Retention.** Data is kept on your device until you delete it, as described in Where your information is stored and retention.

**No sensitive data, profiling or automated decisions.** We do not ask for sensitive personal data, and we do not carry out profiling or automated decision-making.

**Your rights.** You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing of, and receive a portable copy of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can do all of this directly in the App: edit or delete items, export a backup, or turn off permissions in iOS Settings. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot access or change it for you, but you can contact us with any question.

**Complaints.** You can complain to the data protection authority where you live. EEA: [find your regulator](https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en). UK: [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

**Transfers outside Europe.** We do not transfer your data. Place searches are handled by Apple under its own safeguards for international transfers.
