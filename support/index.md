# habbit Support

Need help with habbit, found a bug, or have an idea? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/tme00/habbit-privacy/issues) and we'll get back to you. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Everything stays on your iPhone and in its iCloud or computer backup. habbit has no accounts and no servers. See the [Privacy Policy](../).

**How do I move my data to a new phone?**
Restoring your iPhone from a backup brings habbit's data with it. You can also go to Profile › Settings › Export Backup, save the file to iCloud Drive or Files, then use Import Backup on the new phone. Importing replaces everything currently in habbit.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Deleted items go to Profile › Settings › Recently Deleted, where you can restore them for 30 days.

**My reminders don't show up.**
Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings › habbit › Notifications, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them.

**How do I add a widget?**
Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and search for habbit.

**How do I import birthdays?**
In the Planner tab, tap Import from Contacts. habbit only reads the names and birthdays of the contacts you pick.
